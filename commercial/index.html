Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Loveland HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Loveland

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Loveland HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Loveland and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
